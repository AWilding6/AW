Hospital Readmissions Quality Analysis | FY2026

MySQL • Tableau • CMS Data

Analyzed 18,330 hospital records from the CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program to identify which conditions, states, and hospitals have the highest excess readmission rates relative to national benchmarks. Key finding: high-volume hospitals consistently outperform low-volume hospitals across all six conditions tracked, with an average excess ratio of 0.963 vs. 1.012 — a difference with real implications for where patients receive care.
